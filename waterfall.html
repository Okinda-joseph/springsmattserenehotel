Welcome to The Waterside Grill the signature dining experience

Welcome to The Waterside Grill, the signature dining experience at SpringsMatt Hotel, where culinary excellence meets serene surroundings. Nestled alongside picturesque springs, our restaurant offers a tranquil and elegant atmosphere perfect for any occasion, from intimate dinners to celebratory gatherings. Our menu showcases a blend of classic and contemporary dishes, crafted from the finest locally sourced ingredients. Savor succulent seafood, premium steaks, and vibrant seasonal produce, all expertly prepared by our talented chefs. Each dish is designed to delight your senses and elevate your dining experience.

 

The Waterside Grill boasts a stylish yet cozy interior with panoramic views of the sparkling springs. Enjoy your meal in our beautifully appointed dining room or opt for an al fresco experience on our scenic terrace, where the soothing sounds of nature create a perfect backdrop for your meal.

 

Delight in our signature dishes such as the Grilled Atlantic Salmon with Lemon Herb Butter, Tenderloin Medallions with Red Wine Reduction, and our Fresh Garden Salad with a Citrus Vinaigrette. Don’t forget to indulge in our decadent desserts, including the famous SpringsMatt Chocolate Lava Cake.

 

 

Complement your meal with a selection from our extensive wine list, featuring both local and international vintages. Our expert sommeliers are on hand to help you find the perfect pairing. We also offer a variety of craft beers, artisanal cocktails, and refreshing non-alcoholic beverages. At The Waterside Grill, we pride ourselves on delivering impeccable service. Our friendly and knowledgeable staff are dedicated to ensuring your dining experience is nothing short of extraordinary.

 

Join us at The Waterside Grill for a dining experience that combines exquisite cuisine, unparalleled ambiance, and outstanding service. Whether you’re a guest of SpringsMatt Hotel or a local resident, we look forward to welcoming you and making your visit truly memorable.
